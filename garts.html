I am working on Garts, a mini-golf/darts hybrid where a golf ball earns points based on where it comes to rest. I am developing a computer-vision system that will track the ball, determine its final position, calculate the score, and record the gameplay data automatically. The goal is a seamless game that students can walk in and play throughout Spring 2027 without anyone needing to operate the system.
